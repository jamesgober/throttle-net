# throttle-net v2.0.0 — Correct Under Contention, Safe by Default (DRAFT)

**A correctness and hardening release. Upgrade from 1.0.0 is strongly recommended.** Under ordinary multi-threaded use, 1.0.0's token buckets admitted 25–180 times their configured rate — the exact overload the crate exists to prevent. 2.0.0 fixes that and every finding of two independent audits, and makes the defaults fail safe: a server's `Retry-After` cannot stall you for years, a key flood cannot reset anyone's per-key limit, one tenant cannot starve the others through a shared scope, and a misspelt dimension is refused instead of free. It is a major release because several of those defaults change behaviour that 1.x documented; the API names and call shapes from 1.0 remain, and the MSRV stays at Rust 1.85.

## Highlights

- **No over-admission under contention.** The bucket engine is better-bucket 1.1, whose state is a single instant that a stale clock reading can only make stricter; real-clock stress tests from 2 to 16 threads hold every limiter to its bound.
- **Fair, cancel-safe waiting with real deadlines.** Token-bucket waiters reserve and are served in order; composites queue on their bottleneck so a shared scope is never held ahead of use; every `*_timeout` bounds the whole wait; pauses hold every waiter and `resume` wakes them.
- **Bounded memory that never resets a limited key.** A unique-key flood costs ~0.36 µs per key (1.0: ~1.8 ms), and a limited key evicted to make room keeps its debt in a compact ledger.
- **Untrusted server input is capped.** Provider reconciliation pauses for at most five minutes by default, and `Retry` honours a `Retry-After` of at most five minutes.
- **Readiness-based `tower` layers.** The throttle, breaker, and adaptive layers wait in `poll_ready`, so `load_shed`, `buffer`, and balancers see real backpressure.

## Breaking changes

Every item below has before/after code in [`docs/MIGRATION_1_TO_2.md`](../MIGRATION_1_TO_2.md).

### `Retry` caps an honoured `Retry-After` at five minutes

A longer server delay now ends the retry loop with the error instead of sleeping. **Why:** a hostile `Retry-After: 999999999` parked a 1.0 loop for 31 years. **Migration:** `Retry::max_retry_after(d)` chooses the ceiling; `Duration::MAX` restores 1.0.

### A full `PerKey` store never resets a limited key

1.0 evicted the least-recently-seen key, whatever its state, so a flood of unique keys could hand a limited key a fresh allowance. 2.0 evicts recovered keys first; a limited key goes only into a per-shard debt ledger and returns in its debt; when keys and ledger are both full of limited keys, a new key is refused until one recovers. **Migration:** size `Eviction::capacity` above your key cardinality; `Eviction::with_when_full(WhenFull::ForgetDebt)` trades exactness for never refusing a newcomer.

### Provider reconciliation pauses, within a cap

`RateLimitInfo::sync_requests` / `sync_tokens` now also pause the limiter for a `Retry-After`, and until the reset when a window is exhausted (1.0 only drained). Every server-driven pause is clamped to `provider::DEFAULT_MAX_PAUSE` (five minutes). **Migration:** `sync_requests_capped(&limiter, Duration::ZERO)` restores drain-only; any other ceiling works too.

### `MultiLimiter` refuses unknown dimension names

A cost naming a dimension the limiter lacks is refused (`Decision::Impossible`, `ThrottleError::UnknownDimension`) instead of charging nothing; repeated names in one call are summed. **Migration:** fix the names, or build with `.strict(false)`.

### Waiting acquires reserve; composites queue on their bottleneck

Waiters take their tokens up front and sleep until they accrue, so `try_acquire` and `available()` see queued waiters' debt, and under a `ManualClock` a waiting acquire returns after its (runtime) sleep rather than looping until the clock is advanced. **Migration:** drive waiting tests with `TokioClock` on tokio's paused time.

### `Retry` and three builders are `Clone`, not `Copy`

`CircuitBreakerBuilder`, `AdaptiveLimiterBuilder`, `QueueBuilder`, and `Retry` lost `Copy` because their `name` now takes an owned name (`impl Into<Cow<'static, str>>`) like every other builder. **Migration:** `.clone()` where a value was reused after a copy.

### Trait, error, and metrics details

- `Limiter` gained provided methods (`paused_for`, `resume`, `charge`, alongside `refund`, `reserve`, `pause_for`) and blanket impls for `&L`, `Box<L>`, and `Arc<L>`; a downstream blanket impl may now overlap.
- `ThrottleError` gained `BulkheadFull`, `InvalidConfig`, `UnknownDimension`, and `NotReady` (the enum is `#[non_exhaustive]`).
- Metrics go through the `metrics` 0.24 facade (1.0: 0.23) and carry a `name` label.
- An AIMD limit falls once per congestion event, also for custom `AdaptiveStrategy` implementations.

## Security

- **Rate-limit bypass under concurrency (TN-001).** better-bucket 1.0 reused a stale clock reading in its compare-and-swap loop: `Throttle::per_second(1000)` granted ~77,000 tokens in two seconds on four threads (the bound is about 3,000). Every bucket-backed type inherited it; the floor is now better-bucket 1.1.
- **Limit reset through eviction (TN-011, TN2-007).** Capacity eviction (1.0) and idle-TTL reclamation of keys in reservation debt (the unpublished 1.1 candidate) handed limited keys a fresh allowance. Neither can now.
- **Per-key CPU denial of service (TN-003).** A new key past the cap scanned its whole shard under the write lock; eviction is now bounded work per insert.
- **Unbounded server-driven waits (TN-013, TN2-008).** A `Retry-After` or reset from a response can no longer pause a client or a retry loop for longer than its ceiling (five minutes by default).
- **Key-flood lockout halved and documented (TN2-011).** Locking new keys out needs a flood that keeps twice the cap of keys drained; the `Eviction` docs give the rates per limit.

## Fixed

- `Bulkhead` and `AdaptiveLimiter` waiters no longer hang with a slot free when two slots free before the first woken waiter runs (TN2-001).
- A tenant queued on its own slow scope no longer drains a shared `Layered` global scope or `Hybrid` constituent ahead of use, starving other tenants (TN2-002).
- A `CircuitBreaker` inside a composite no longer wedges half-open after a rollback or cancellation (TN2-003); manual outcomes no longer free trial slots they never held (TN2-015); queued waiters are shed if the breaker opens during their wait (TN2-013).
- `sync_requests(&arc_throttle)` compiles again (TN2-004).
- A timed acquire no longer waits forever while a constituent answers "retry now" (TN2-006).
- A pause holds waiters that already reserved, and `resume` wakes paused waiters (TN2-009, TN2-010).
- `settle_costs` charges an overrun even while a dimension is paused (TN2-016); a sliding-log constituent is charged when the request goes (TN2-014).
- Clippy is clean on Rust 1.85 (CI's MSRV leg failed on `comparison_chain`) and on Rust 1.99 (TN2-005).
- From the 1.0 audit: a cancelled probe no longer wedges a breaker (TN-002); late outcomes no longer settle a later state (TN-008); adaptive updates are no longer lost (TN-006) and correlated failures no longer collapse the limit (TN-007); sub-millisecond windows (TN-009), large capacities (TN-010), slow rates (TN-020), sliding overflow (TN-019), the backoff panic (TN-012), OpenAI preset over-admission (TN-033), composite unit leaks (TN-022), large-cost starvation (TN-023), provider reset handling (TN-017, TN-018), metrics wiring (TN-014, TN-015, TN-030), and the `std`-only and `thumbv7em` builds (TN-025).

## Added

- **Waiting surface.** `*_timeout` on every limiter; `acquire_owned_timeout` on `CircuitBreaker`, `AdaptiveLimiter`, and `Bulkhead`; `Bulkhead::call_timeout`; `resume` / `paused_for` on `Hybrid`, `MultiLimiter`, and `Layered`.
- **`Limiter`.** `refund`, `reserve`, `pause_for`, `paused_for`, `resume`, and `charge`, with conservative defaults; impls for `&L`, `Box<L>`, `Arc<L>`.
- **`Throttle`.** A builder with independent `burst` and `initial` fill, `per_minute`, `smooth`, `reserve`, `refund`, `drain_to`, `pause_for` / `resume` / `paused_for`.
- **Retries.** `Retry::max_retry_after`, `max_elapsed`, and `RetryBudget` with `run_with_budget`.
- **`MultiLimiter`.** Aliases, strict names, `try_build`, and `settle_costs` to admit an LLM call on its `max_tokens` estimate and settle with real usage.
- **`PerKey`.** A builder, `reserve`, `refund`, `reset`, `remove`, `clear`; the debt ledger and `WhenFull`.
- **Resilience.** `Bulkhead`; `resilience::{timeout, fallback, hedge}`; `CircuitBreaker::call`, owned permits, `cancel()` to give a permit back without an outcome, and a `Limiter` impl; adaptive owned permits, `cancel()`, `release`, and a `Limiter` impl.
- **Providers.** `Window::apply`, `RateLimitInfo::sync_multi`, the `*_capped` variants, and `provider::DEFAULT_MAX_PAUSE`.
- **Integration.** The `tower` feature (`ThrottleLayer`, `CircuitBreakerLayer`, `AdaptiveLayer`, each with `with_timeout`) and `TokioClock`.
- **Observability.** Instance names on every metric; denial, timeout, shed, queue-rejection, retry, and retry-budget counters.

## Performance

Criterion means on Windows 11 x86_64, rustc 1.95.0, on a shared development machine (indicative); 1.0.0 was measured on the same machine.

| Operation | 1.0.0 | 2.0.0 |
|---|---|---|
| `Throttle::try_acquire` | 36.0 ns | 35.3 ns |
| `Throttle::reserve` + `refund` | — | 39.2 ns |
| `PerKey::try_acquire`, 10,000 keys | 100.3 ns | 51.4 ns |
| `Hybrid` of two buckets | — | 140.6 ns |
| `MultiLimiter`, three dimensions | — | 315.9 ns |
| `PerKey` new key at the default 1M-key cap | ~1.8 ms | 0.36 µs |
| `PerKey` new key, keys and ledger full of limited keys (refused) | — | 0.52 µs |
| `PerKey` insert with an idle TTL, nothing expired | ~26 µs, growing | 0.17 µs |

`governor` 0.10 measured 8.6 ns on the same machine: it is GCRA over one timestamp, while a `Throttle` reads the OS monotonic clock on every call.

## Known issues

- `Hybrid` and `MultiLimiter` read the clock once per constituent for the peek and again for the commit (~140 ns for two buckets, ~316 ns for three dimensions); a single-reading path is planned.
- `SlidingWindowLog` cannot reserve: its waiters retry as the window frees, so under sustained contention a large cost can wait behind smaller ones, and `refund` removes the oldest grants (never more permissive than the exact refund). Use a token bucket where arrival-order fairness matters.
- `resume()` on any limiter wakes every waiter that is sleeping out a pause, process-wide; each re-checks its own limiter and sleeps again if it is still paused.
- Uncontended `try_acquire` is ~35 ns against `governor`'s ~9 ns (the monotonic clock read).
- The `tower` layers charge a fixed cost per request; a per-request cost function is planned.

## Migration guide

1. Set `throttle-net = "2"` in `Cargo.toml` and recompile.
2. Decide on the new defaults where they differ from 1.x: `Retry::max_retry_after`, `Eviction::with_when_full`, `sync_*_capped`, and `MultiLimiter::strict` (each restores 1.x if you need it).
3. Replace copies of `Retry` and the breaker, adaptive, and queue builders with `.clone()`.
4. Install a `metrics` 0.24 recorder if you use the `metrics` feature, and name your instances.
5. Run your tests; tests that advanced a `ManualClock` to release a waiting acquire should use `TokioClock` with tokio's paused time.

The full guide, with before/after code for every change: [`docs/MIGRATION_1_TO_2.md`](../MIGRATION_1_TO_2.md).
